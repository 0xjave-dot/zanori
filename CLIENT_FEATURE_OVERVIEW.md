# Zanori Spaces Website Feature Overview

## Client Presentation Summary

This website presents Zanori Spaces as a premium interior design and lifestyle brand through a polished, interactive digital experience. It combines storytelling, design presentation, service consultation, retail browsing, client account management, and administrative control into one cohesive platform tailored for a luxury brand experience.

---

## 1. Premium Brand Presentation

The website is designed to immediately communicate elegance, confidence, and refinement.

- A strong visual-first homepage that establishes the brand identity from the first impression
- Luxury-inspired typography, layout, and color palette
- Carefully designed transitions, motion effects, and layered visual depth
- Clear calls to action that guide visitors toward consultation, portfolio exploration, or shopping
- A tone and structure that align well with a high-end interior design business

---

## 2. Portfolio and Project Storytelling

The website gives Zanori Spaces a professional and visually compelling way to showcase completed work.

- Dedicated portfolio sections for featured projects
- Project-based presentation that highlights design thinking and execution
- Visual storytelling that helps clients understand the studio’s approach to spaces
- A structure that builds credibility by turning completed work into a strong design narrative
- A presentation format that is ideal for attracting new clients and demonstrating capability

---

## 3. Interior Services and Consultation Experience

The services section is built to make the studio’s offerings clear, attractive, and easy to act on.

- Clear presentation of interior and design-related services
- A structured consultation journey for prospective clients
- A project estimate tool that helps clients understand budget considerations
- Custom consultation brief generation to support lead follow-up
- A smooth path from interest to inquiry, helping the business convert visitors into clients

---

## 4. Boutique Furniture and Retail Experience

The website includes a curated shopping experience that feels refined and premium rather than generic.

- Product browsing by category for a more intuitive retail experience
- Search and filtering tools to help clients discover suitable pieces quickly
- Price-based filtering for budget awareness and decision support
- Wishlist functionality for saving favorite items
a- Product inquiry options for a more conversational retail process
- Gift purchase functionality for special occasions and client gifting

---

## 5. Client-Centered Personal Space

The website includes a private client experience that makes the platform feel more personal and valuable over time.

- Secure account creation and login functionality
- Google authentication support for convenience
- A personal saved-design area for clients to preserve inspiration
- Wishlist management for future purchases or design planning
- Gift purchase history and account-level activity for a more tailored experience- Optional future enhancement: AI-assisted room reimagination can be introduced at the client’s request as an additional feature set
---

## 6. Content Management and Business Control

The site is not only a public-facing experience but also a practical business tool.

- An administrator section for updating portfolio content
- The ability to create and manage product listings in the shop
- Image upload and content editing functionality
- A flexible structure that allows the business to keep the website current as offerings evolve
- Built-in controls that support ongoing website maintenance without needing a full rebuild

---

## 7. Inquiry and Contact Conversion System

The website is designed to support real business outcomes by turning visitors into leads.

- Consultation modal for direct client communication
- Inquiry draft support for selected products and services
- Contact form flow for project and service inquiries
- WhatsApp and communication support features for convenient follow-up
- A clear conversion funnel that helps the studio capture interest efficiently

---

## 8. Responsive and Premium User Experience

The website has been built to feel polished and functional across different devices and screen sizes.

- Mobile-friendly navigation and layout
- Smooth transitions and interactive feedback throughout the site
- A refined visual experience that remains consistent across desktop and mobile
- Modern presentation standards that support both brand perception and usability
- A strong balance between aesthetic presentation and practical functionality

---

## 9. Strategic Business Value

From a business perspective, the website acts as more than a digital brochure.

- It elevates the brand image of Zanori Spaces
- It creates a professional channel for presenting design work
- It supports lead generation through consultation and inquiry flows
- It adds a retail layer for product discovery and customer engagement
- It provides a scalable digital platform that can grow with the business

---

## Summary

This website positions Zanori Spaces as a modern, premium, and highly capable interior design brand. It combines visual storytelling, service clarity, commerce, client interaction, and content management into a single experience that is both beautiful and functional.

In practical terms, the website helps Zanori Spaces:

- Present the business with elegance and authority
- Showcase design expertise and completed work
- Encourage consultations and project enquiries
- Support product discovery and customer engagement
- Create a professional digital foundation for long-term growth
