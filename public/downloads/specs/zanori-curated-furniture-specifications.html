Specification reference sheet for the launch catalog. Retail and sale prices are shown in Nigerian Naira and may vary by availability, finish, and freight.

Product specification policy

Dimensions are supplied in centimetres. Materials and colourways are the selected catalogue options. Stock is an indicative showroom allocation and should be confirmed with Zanori before purchase.

Source and authenticity

Each item in the digital catalog carries its manufacturer, SKU where supported, and a source link to the manufacturer's product or collection page. Zanori's team should confirm lead time and final finish before issuing a quotation.

Care and delivery

Keep upholstered items away from prolonged direct sun and moisture. Solid timber should be acclimatised indoors before installation. Outdoor pieces should be installed under the care instructions supplied by the manufacturer.
