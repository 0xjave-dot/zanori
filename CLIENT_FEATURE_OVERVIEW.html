Zanori Spaces

Website Feature Overview for Client Presentation
This document outlines the key features of the Zanori Spaces website and explains how the platform supports brand presentation, client engagement, design consultation, shopping, and business growth in a polished digital experience.

1. Premium Brand Presentation

  • A strong visual-first homepage that establishes the brand identity immediately
  • Luxury-inspired typography, layout, and color palette
  • Refined motion effects and layered visual depth
  • Clear calls to action guiding visitors toward consultation, portfolio browsing, or shopping

2. Portfolio and Project Storytelling

  • Dedicated portfolio sections for featured projects
  • Project-based presentation that highlights design thinking and execution
  • Visual storytelling that helps clients understand the studio’s approach to spaces
  • A presentation format that builds credibility and reinforces design capability

3. Interior Services and Consultation Experience

  • Clear presentation of interior and design-related services
  • A structured consultation journey for prospective clients
  • A project estimate tool that supports budget conversations
  • Custom consultation brief generation to support lead follow-up

4. Boutique Furniture and Retail Experience

  • Curated product browsing with category-based navigation
  • Search and filtering tools for easier discovery
  • Price-based filtering to support decision-making
  • Wishlist functionality and gift purchase options for a richer client experience

5. Client-Centered Personal Space

  • Secure account creation and login functionality
  • Google authentication support for convenience
  • A personal saved-design area for preserving inspiration
  • Wishlist management and account-level activity for a more tailored experience
  • Optional future enhancement: AI-assisted room reimagination can be introduced at the client’s request as an additional feature set

6. Featured Design Showcase

  • A dedicated showcase area for premium design assets that clients can browse
  • Support for both plain design files and 3D design assets
  • Flexible access options where items can be offered for purchase or made available for free
  • Admin panel upload capability for adding, updating, and managing design showcase items
  • A simple way to present downloadable or purchasable design content in a polished, branded format

7. Content Management and Business Control

  • An administrator section for updating portfolio content
  • The ability to create and manage product listings in the shop
  • Image upload and content editing functionality
  • Support for managing featured design showcase assets from the admin panel
  • A flexible system that helps the business keep the website current as offerings grow

8. Inquiry and Contact Conversion System

  • Consultation modal for direct client communication
  • Inquiry draft support for selected products and services
  • Contact form flow for project and service inquiries
  • Communication support features that help convert interest into action

9. Responsive and Premium User Experience

  • Mobile-friendly navigation and layout
  • Smooth transitions and interactive feedback throughout the site
  • A consistent premium experience across desktop and mobile
  • A strong balance between aesthetic presentation and functional usability

10. Strategic Business Value

  • Elevates the brand image of Zanori Spaces
  • Provides a professional channel for presenting design work
  • Supports lead generation through consultation and inquiry flows
  • Adds a retail and engagement layer that can scale with business growth
Overall value: The website positions Zanori Spaces as a modern, premium, and highly capable interior design brand that combines visual storytelling, service clarity, commerce, and client interaction in one polished experience.
